Hi, I'm Favour — welcome in.

I'm a young creator learning to build things, both in code and in life. This blog is where I keep the honest, unfiltered version of that process: the wins, the confusing parts, and everything I'm slowly figuring out along the way.

I care about coding, content creation, video editing, creativity, personal growth and my faith — and most of my posts land somewhere in the overlap of those things.

My Journey

A rough, honest timeline of how this blog — and this season of my life — came together.

2021

Started creating content just for fun

No plan, no strategy — just a phone camera and a lot of curiosity about telling stories online.

2022

Fell in love with editing

What started as trimming clips turned into genuinely enjoying the craft of putting a video together.

2023

Wrote my first line of code

A single HTML heading tag that shouldn't have felt like a big deal, but absolutely did.

2024

Started this blog

A space to bring everything — the coding, the content, the faith, the growth — together in one honest place.
